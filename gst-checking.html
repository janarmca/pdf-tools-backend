How matching works: Tier 1 — same GSTIN, invoice no., date and taxable value → Matched. Tier 2 — same GSTIN + invoice no. but the taxable value differs → Amount difference. Tier 3 — same GSTIN and a taxable value within tolerance, but invoice no./date differ → Invoice No & Date difference. Tier 4 — the same invoice no. and a close taxable value appear on both sides but under different GSTINs → GSTIN mismatch (a common real exception: the vendor's GSTIN was mistyped in the books, or the vendor filed under a different registration). Anything left over is genuinely missing on one side — Bills booked in books but not reflected in GSTR‑2B or Bills in GSTR‑2B but not in books.

Column mapping: GSTIN, Invoice No., Date and Taxable Value are required on both sides. Party Name and Tax Amount are optional — if your GSTR‑2B export splits tax into Integrated/Central/State/Cess columns, add a single "Total Tax" column to that file before uploading, or leave Tax Amount unmapped and the tool will reconcile on taxable value alone.

GSTIN check: every GSTIN is validated against the official 15‑character format and checksum digit — a red ⚠ means it's malformed or the checksum fails, worth confirming with the vendor before claiming ITC against it.

Exported workbook: "Export Books vs GSTR-2B workbook" builds the same three-sheet report your CA expects — Books vs GSTR-2B Reconciliation (line-by-line, with live difference formulas and a TOTAL row), Executive Summary (category-wise COUNTIF/SUMIF totals), and Group Summary (Vendor-wise) (GSTIN-wise rollup with an Action Needed column, sorted by tax amount at risk).
