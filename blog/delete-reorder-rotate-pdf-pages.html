How to Delete, Reorder and Rotate PDF Pages Online Free

5 min read · Updated October 2026

A blank page at the end, a page upside down, an appendix that should come first. These are the three small fixes that make a PDF presentable, and none of them needs expensive software.

Delete unwanted pages

Open Delete Pages, add your PDF and tap each page you want to remove — it gets a red outline. A bar at the top tells you how many are marked and how many will remain. Use Select all or Clear if that is quicker, then press Delete & Download. You cannot delete every page, because that would leave an empty file.

Reorder pages

In Reorder Pages, each page has ◀ and ▶ buttons. Press ▶ to move a page one step later and ◀ one step earlier; the page numbers update immediately. When the sequence looks right, press Reorder & Download.

Rotate sideways or upside-down pages

Rotate Pages turns a single page by 90° each time you press its button, so you can fix only the scanned pages that came out wrong. The rest stay as they are.

A good order of operations

  1. Rotate first, so you can recognise every page.
  2. Delete what you do not need.
  3. Reorder last, when only the pages you want are left.
Tip: Use the 🔍 button to zoom in on a page before you delete it. Two scanned pages often look almost identical in a small preview.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting a page change the other pages?

No. The remaining pages are copied unchanged into a new file; your original stays untouched on your device.

Can I undo a delete?

Before you press Download, tap the page again to unmark it. After downloading, simply run the tool again on your original file.

Why is my PDF a little smaller after deleting pages?

Because the removed pages no longer take space in the new file.
