How to Reduce a Photo to a Specific Size in KB for Online Forms

5 min read · Updated October 2026

"File size must be less than 50 KB." Your phone photo is 3 MB. Instead of guessing quality settings again and again, let the tool search for the best quality that still fits the limit.

Read the portal's rules first

Every form has its own limits — a minimum and maximum size in KB, sometimes exact pixel dimensions, and a format such as JPG. The numbers differ between exams, jobs and schemes, so always follow what that form states.

Compress to the target size

  1. Open Compress to Target KB.
  2. Add your photo or signature image.
  3. Enter the maximum size the form allows.
  4. The tool tries different quality levels automatically and gives you the best one under the limit.
  5. Download and upload to the form.

Keep the picture useful

  • Start from the original photo, not one that was already compressed several times.
  • Crop to the required framing first; a tighter crop needs less compression.
  • If the face or signature turns blurry, the limit may be too tight for that image — retake the photo with plain light and background.

Related fixes

Need to resize in pixels? Use the image resize tool. Need a document in PDF form? See converting photos to a PDF and then compressing it.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my image become blurry?

Lower KB limits force stronger compression. Crop first and use a sharp original.

Which format is best?

Use the format the form asks for; JPG is the most common for photos.

Is the image uploaded anywhere?

No, compression runs in your browser.
