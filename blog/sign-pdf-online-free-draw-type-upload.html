How to Sign a PDF Online for Free — Draw, Type or Upload Your Signature

5 min read · Updated October 2026

Printing a form only to sign it and scan it again is a waste of an afternoon. You can place your signature directly on the PDF in under a minute.

Three ways to create the signature

  • Draw — use a finger on a phone or a mouse on a computer; pick the ink colour.
  • Type — type your name and use a handwriting-style font.
  • Upload — add a photo of your real signature on white paper. Crop it closely so there is no extra background.

Sign step by step

  1. Open Sign PDF and drop the document.
  2. Create your signature using one of the three modes.
  3. Place it on the page, resize it to sit on the signature line, and apply.
  4. Download the signed PDF.

What this is — and is not

This adds an image of your signature to the PDF, just like signing on paper and scanning. It is not a digital-certificate signature or an Aadhaar eSign, which verify identity cryptographically. Many everyday forms accept an image signature, but a bank, court or government portal may require a specific kind — check what the recipient asks for.

Tips for a professional look

  • Sign slowly on a white background if you upload a photo.
  • Keep the signature inside the line and do not cover printed text.
  • Save a copy named clearly, for example agreement-signed.pdf.

Frequently asked questions

Is an image signature legally valid?

It depends on the document and who is receiving it. Some accept it, others require a digital signature or eSign. Ask the recipient.

Is my document uploaded?

No. Signing happens in your browser.

Can I sign several pages?

Run the tool on the file again for each page that needs a signature.
