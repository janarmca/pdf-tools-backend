What's New at PDF Tools India — October 2026 Update

4 min read · Updated October 2026

Here is a plain list of what changed on PDF Tools India this month — what you will notice, and why we changed it. Every tool listed here is still free and still runs in your browser.

Split PDF now shows your pages

Earlier, after you added a PDF you only saw the file name and the number of pages. Now every page appears as a preview. Tap a page to add it to (or remove it from) the range, or press the 🔍 button on any page to open a large, sharp view and move between pages with ◀ ▶. The page-range box fills itself in, so you do not have to type "2-3, 7" by hand — although you still can.

Delete Pages, Reorder Pages and Rotate Pages no longer jump

These three tools used to redraw every page after each click, which sent you back to the top of a long PDF. They now update only the page you touched, so your place on the screen stays put. In Delete Pages you can tap anywhere on a page card to mark it; a bar at the top shows how many pages are marked and how many will remain, with Select all, Clear and Delete & Download buttons always within reach.

Kalvi Kalanjiyam: learning through doing

Our free learning area for Tamil students (Classes 1–10) added a hands-on Experiments Lab with 28 experiments, 11 "hit the target" mission games, and 27 science and real-life maths games for Classes 6–10. Every subject page now opens with a short "Why do we learn this?" note, and a new page explains what Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology actually are. Everything is in Tamil and English.

What did not change

No watermarks, and for the tools that run in the browser your files are processed on your own device. AI tools are different, because they need to send your text to a server to get an answer, so read the tool page before using one with confidential content.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to clear my browser to see the updates?

Usually not. If an old version still shows, refresh the page once or close and reopen the tab.

Are the new features free?

Yes. Page previews, Delete, Reorder, Rotate and Kalvi Kalanjiyam are free to use.
