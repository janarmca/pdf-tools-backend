How to play

  1. Pick what to type and how long the race lasts, then press Start Race.
  2. Type the highlighted word and press Space. The timer starts on your first key.
  3. Your car 🏎️ moves as you type correctly. Beat the 🚙 — it drives at your best speed so far (or 20 WPM on your first race).
  4. Earn XP, level up and collect badges. Accuracy counts — XP drops when you make lots of mistakes.

Tamil words (Tanglish)

You see the Tamil word, its English-letter spelling and its meaning. Type the English-letter spelling (for example அம்மா → amma). This builds the skill for Tamil phone keyboards that turn English letters into Tamil.

Tips

  • Look at the screen, not the keyboard. Keep fingers on the home row: A S D F and J K L ;
  • Accuracy first, speed will follow. 95%+ accuracy is a great goal.
  • WPM = correct letters ÷ 5, per minute (the standard way typing speed is measured).

🔒 Everything runs in your browser. Your progress is saved only on this device.
