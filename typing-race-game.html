About this tool

Type Race is part of PDF Tools India's Education toolkit. It's a self-paced typing speed and accuracy practice game covering English words and sentences as well as Tamil words typed in Tanglish, with its own XP, level, and badge system to keep practice fun. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server. Available in Tamil and English, on both mobile and desktop, with no account required to try it.

How it works

  1. Click "Open Type Race" above.
  2. Pick English or Tamil words/sentences and start typing as they appear.
  3. See your speed and accuracy instantly, and earn XP and badges as you improve.
