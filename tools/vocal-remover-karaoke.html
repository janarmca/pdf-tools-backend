இந்தக் கருவி பற்றி About this tool

Uses centre-channel cancellation with the bass kept — works on studio songs where the voice is in the centre. Vocal Remover (Karaoke) is part of PDF Tools India's Video toolkit. Remove the singer from a stereo song or music video — or keep only the voice. Free by default, with an optional paid Fast Server upgrade for large files. Available in Tamil and English, on both mobile and desktop, with no account required to try it.

🔒 Free by default, with an optional paid Fast Server upgrade for large files.

எப்படிப் பயன்படுத்துவது? How it works

  1. Open Vocal Remover and choose a song or video.
  2. Choose Remove vocals or Keep vocals.
  3. Process & download (MP3, WAV or video).

எதற்குப் பயன்படும்? Common uses

  • Karaoke for a function
  • Practising a song
  • Instrumental for a video

Does it work on every song?

Best on stereo studio songs; mono recordings or live recordings cannot be separated without AI.

Is Vocal Remover (Karaoke) free to use?

Yes. Vocal Remover (Karaoke) is free on PDF Tools India — no signup, no watermark and no daily limit.

Is my data safe when I use Vocal Remover (Karaoke)?

Most processing happens in your browser. Heavy video jobs can optionally use a faster server, and files there are deleted after processing.

Does Vocal Remover (Karaoke) work on mobile?

Yes. Vocal Remover (Karaoke) works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and desktop browsers. The interface is available in Tamil and English.
