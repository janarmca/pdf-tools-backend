இந்தக் கருவி பற்றி About this tool

Upload a photo of a person, product or logo and the background is cut out automatically. You can download it with a transparent background, fill it with a solid colour (white for passport and ID photos, for example), or place the subject on another picture, then touch up the edges by hand if needed. Remove Background is part of PDF Tools India's Image toolkit. remove.bg-quality AI cutout + background color/photo swap + touch-up. Runs entirely in your browser — your photo is never uploaded to a server. Available in Tamil and English, on both mobile and desktop, with no account required to try it.

எப்படிப் பயன்படுத்துவது? How it works

  1. Open the tool and choose a JPG, PNG or WEBP photo.
  2. Wait a few seconds while the background is removed automatically.
  3. Pick transparent, a solid colour or a new background photo, and fix any rough edges with the touch-up brush.
  4. Download the result as a PNG.

எதற்குப் பயன்படும்? Common uses

  • Product photos for online shops and WhatsApp catalogues
  • White or blue background for passport, visa and ID photos
  • Profile pictures and thumbnails
  • Logos and signatures on transparent PNG

அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள் FAQ

Can I remove the background from a PDF?

Convert the PDF page to an image first with the PDF → Image tool, then remove its background here and, if you like, turn the result back into a PDF with Image → PDF.

Does the result keep a transparent background?

Yes. Download the PNG and the background stays transparent, so you can place it on any slide, poster or document.

Is Remove Background free to use?

Yes. Remove Background is free on PDF Tools India — no signup, no watermark and no daily limit.

Is my data safe when I use Remove Background?

Yes. Remove Background runs inside your own browser, so your files and text never leave your device.

Does Remove Background work on mobile?

Yes. Remove Background works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and desktop browsers. The interface is available in Tamil and English.
