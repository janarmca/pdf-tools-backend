இந்தக் கருவி பற்றி About this tool

Turn a picture of a table into a real Excel sheet. Upload or paste (Ctrl+V) a screenshot of an Excel sheet, a bank statement, a bill or a photo of a printed table. The tool straightens tilted photos, removes grid lines that confuse text reading, fixes dark-mode and white-on-green headers, then places every value in the right row and column. Numbers (₹, %, 1,23,456.00), dates and text are recognised and written as proper Excel cells with a styled header, filters and optional TOTAL formulas. Image → Excel is part of PDF Tools India's Image toolkit. Screenshot or photo of a table → editable .xlsx. Fixes grid lines, dark mode, tilted photos; cleans numbers & dates — free, in your browser. Runs entirely in your browser — your photo is never uploaded to a server. Available in Tamil and English, on both mobile and desktop, with no account required to try it.

எப்படிப் பயன்படுத்துவது? How it works

  1. Open Image → Excel and drop a screenshot or photo of the table (or press Ctrl+V to paste).
  2. Choose the language if it is not English, then tap Convert to Excel.
  3. Check the preview: yellow cells are the ones to double-check; click any cell to correct it.
  4. Download the .xlsx (or CSV), or copy the table straight into Excel or Google Sheets.

எதற்குப் பயன்படும்? Common uses

  • Moving a table from a screenshot or WhatsApp image into Excel
  • Bank statements, bills and price lists received as pictures
  • Printed marks lists, registers and invoices
  • Combining several screenshots into one sheet

அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள் FAQ

Is it free and private?

Yes. It runs in your browser with open-source text reading — no AI credits, no signup, and your image is never uploaded to a server.

Will it be 100% exact?

Clear screenshots come out almost perfectly. Blurry photos can misread a few characters, so uncertain cells are highlighted yellow for a quick check before you download.

Does it handle dates and Indian number format?

Yes. Dates like 01/10/2026 become real Excel dates (day-first or month-first, your choice) and amounts like 1,23,456.00 become numbers with Indian grouping.

Is Image → Excel free to use?

Yes. Image → Excel is free on PDF Tools India — no signup, no watermark and no daily limit.

Is my data safe when I use Image → Excel?

Yes. Image → Excel runs inside your own browser, so your files and text never leave your device.

Does Image → Excel work on mobile?

Yes. Image → Excel works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and desktop browsers. The interface is available in Tamil and English.

What does Image → Excel do?
