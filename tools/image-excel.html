About this tool

Turn a picture of a table into a real Excel sheet. Upload or paste (Ctrl+V) a screenshot of an Excel sheet, a bank statement, a bill or a photo of a printed table. The tool straightens tilted photos, removes grid lines that confuse text reading, fixes dark-mode and white-on-green headers, then places every value in the right row and column. Numbers (₹, %, 1,23,456.00), dates and text are recognised and written as proper Excel cells with a styled header, filters and optional TOTAL formulas. How it works

  1. Open Image → Excel and drop a screenshot or photo of the table (or press Ctrl+V to paste).
  2. Choose the language if it is not English, then tap Convert to Excel.
  3. Check the preview: yellow cells are the ones to double-check; click any cell to correct it.
  4. Download the .xlsx (or CSV), or copy the table straight into Excel or Google Sheets.

Common uses

  • Moving a table from a screenshot or WhatsApp image into Excel
  • Bank statements, bills and price lists received as pictures
  • Printed marks lists, registers and invoices
  • Combining several screenshots into one sheet
