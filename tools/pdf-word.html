Frequently asked questions

Will the layout be identical?

Simple text documents convert very closely. Complex layouts with many columns, tables or images may need small manual adjustments in Word.

What about scanned PDFs?

A scanned PDF is a picture of text. Use Image → Text (OCR) first to read the text, then paste it into Word.
