இந்தக் கருவி பற்றி About this tool

A PAN has 5 letters, 4 digits and 1 letter; the fourth letter shows the holder type (P individual, C company, F firm, H HUF and so on). A TAN has 4 letters, 5 digits and 1 letter. Paste a list, even a column copied from Excel, and every number is checked and explained. Download the result as CSV. PAN / TAN Checker is part of PDF Tools India's Business toolkit. எப்படிப் பயன்படுத்துவது? How it works

  1. Paste PAN / TAN numbers, one per line.
  2. Read the result table: format OK, wrong, duplicate.
  3. Fix the numbers marked wrong; letter O versus digit 0 mistakes are pointed out.
  4. Download the CSV or copy the valid list.

எதற்குப் பயன்படும்? Common uses

  • Cleaning a deductee list before a TDS return
  • Finding O/0 and I/1 typing mistakes
  • Spotting duplicate PANs in a vendor list
  • Telling a company PAN from an individual PAN

அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள் FAQ

Does this confirm that a PAN exists?

No. It checks the format only. To confirm a PAN exists and belongs to a name, use Verify PAN on the Income Tax e-filing portal or TRACES.

Is my list uploaded?

No. It is checked inside your browser.

Is PAN / TAN Checker free to use?

Yes. PAN / TAN Checker is free on PDF Tools India — no signup, no watermark and no daily limit.

Is my data safe when I use PAN / TAN Checker?

Most processing happens in your browser. Heavy video jobs can optionally use a faster server, and files there are deleted after processing.

Does PAN / TAN Checker work on mobile?

Yes. PAN / TAN Checker works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and desktop browsers. The interface is available in Tamil and English.
