இந்தக் கருவி பற்றி About this tool

Upload a signed PDF such as Form 16, a TRACES certificate, a GST or e-filing document, or an Aadhaar e-Sign PDF. The tool reads the signature inside the file and tells you in plain language whether the signature is intact, whether the content was changed after signing, who signed it, when the certificate is valid and which CA issued it. The file stays on your device. DSC / e-Sign Verifier is part of PDF Tools India's PDF toolkit. எப்படிப் பயன்படுத்துவது? How it works

  1. Open DSC / e-Sign Verifier and drop the signed PDF.
  2. Read the green, yellow or red result at the top.
  3. Open each signature card for the signer, issuer, validity dates and certificate chain.
  4. If it says the document changed after signing, download the original again from the source website and upload that without editing.

எதற்குப் பயன்படும்? Common uses

  • Checking a Form 16 or TRACES PDF before filing
  • Finding out why a portal says signature invalid
  • Confirming an e-Signed agreement was not edited
  • Checking whether a DSC certificate had expired when the PDF was signed

அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள் FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The check runs inside your browser and the file never leaves your device.

Does a green tick mean the government portal will accept it?

It means the signature is mathematically intact and the signed content is unchanged. Certificate revocation (OCSP/CRL) and the official trust list cannot be checked offline, so for an official verdict also open the file in Adobe Acrobat Reader.

Can this tool sign a PDF with my USB DSC token?

No. A browser cannot access a USB token by itself; signing needs your token driver and your CA signer utility. See DSC Token Help for fixes.

Why did my signature become invalid?

Any edit, compression, merge, rotate, print-to-PDF or re-save after signing breaks a digital signature. Make all changes first and sign last.

Is DSC / e-Sign Verifier free to use?

Yes. DSC / e-Sign Verifier is free on PDF Tools India — no signup, no watermark and no daily limit.

Is my data safe when I use DSC / e-Sign Verifier?

Yes. DSC / e-Sign Verifier runs inside your own browser, so your files and text never leave your device.

Does DSC / e-Sign Verifier work on mobile?

Yes. DSC / e-Sign Verifier works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and desktop browsers. The interface is available in Tamil and English.
